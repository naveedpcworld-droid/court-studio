# Judge Live-Source Map

Checked: 4 November 2026

This file answers one question: **where does each judge's actual public hearing/stream come from — not the commentary channel that clipped it?**

## The important discovery

Most of these judges are not “on Zoom” through a public Zoom link. Their courtrooms run on Zoom, and the court **broadcasts that Zoom room to YouTube Live**. The dead giveaway is the recurring title:

> “Judge Oakley’s Personal Meeting Room”
>
> “Judge Raquel West’s Personal Meeting Room”
>
> “John Stevens Jr.’s Personal Meeting Room”

So the public original source is usually the court/judge **YouTube Live tab** or the county **CourtStream** page. The actual Zoom meeting ID is generally not published for a trial; the YouTube Live feed is the public feed.

Also: **past live streams are often hidden from `/videos`.** You must open the channel’s **Live** tab or `/streams`. That is why some channels look like they have only 5 videos when they actually have 1,800.

## How to find any other judge’s source

1. Get the exact court from the case caption or judge name (e.g. “187th District Court, Bexar County”).
2. Search YouTube **Channels** for the court name, not the judge’s viral title.
3. Open the channel → **Live** tab or `/streams` — not `/videos`.
4. Look for titles like “Personal Meeting Room”, “Morning Docket”, “State vs …”, or “Zoom Meeting”.
5. Check the About/description for “official”, the county domain, or a “DO NOT RECORD” warning.
6. Check the county’s CourtStream/live page for the public Zoom/YouTube link.
7. If the only high-volume channel is an edited/commentary channel, it is not an original source.
